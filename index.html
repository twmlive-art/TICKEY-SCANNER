Tap Test sounds once at the start of the night. You should hear three different sounds: admit, already in, not on list. On iPhone, check the ringer switch is on and the volume is up.

Update tickets

Loads ticket numbers from TicketsCandy so QR scans show names. Do it on show day (about 4pm) and again if lots of late sales come in.

1. Open TicketsCandy

2. Log in if asked, then tap your Send to door bookmark. It updates every show in the next 3 weeks and gives you a link back here.

First time on this phone: set up the Send to door bookmark

iPhone (Safari): tap Copy below. Bookmark any page (Share, Add Bookmark) and call it Send to door. Then open Bookmarks, Edit, tap that bookmark and paste over the address. Laptop: drag this link to your bookmarks bar: Send to door

The bookmark holds this phone's door PIN. If the PIN changes, make it again.
